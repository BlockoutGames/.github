# Getting help

Where to go, depending on who you are and what happened.

## 🎮 Playing one of our games

| Something went wrong with...         | Do this                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **A bug in a game**                  | Tell us in the [Blockout Games community](https://www.roblox.com/communities/191769851/Blockout-Games) on Roblox. Say which game, what you did and what happened. A screenshot or short video helps a lot. |
| **A purchase you did not receive**   | Rejoin the game first: purchases are saved and granted when you load in. Still missing? Tell us in the community with the game, the item and roughly when you bought it. |
| **A refund or a Robux charge**       | Refunds and Robux billing are handled by Roblox, not by us: [Roblox Support](https://www.roblox.com/support).                                                  |
| **A lost or hacked account**         | Only Roblox can recover an account: [Roblox Support](https://www.roblox.com/support).                                                                         |
| **Another player cheating**          | Use **Report** in the Roblox menu (Esc → Report). Our anti-cheat also flags cheaters automatically.                                                        |
| **An exploit or a way to cheat**     | Please do not post it anywhere public. Follow the [security policy](https://github.com/BlockoutGames/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md) instead.                                                                    |
| **Bad behaviour in chat**            | Use **Report** in the Roblox menu. Roblox moderates chat across every game.                                                                                 |

We will never ask for your password, your Roblox cookie or a "verification" code, and we never
give away Robux. Anyone who claims to be from Blockout Games and asks for these is not us.

## 🛠️ Working on our code

Our repositories are private. If you are a member of the organisation:

- **Something broken or a question about the code?** Open an issue in that repository, using the
  bug or task form.
- **Not sure where something lives?** Start at the repository's `README.md`, then its `docs/`.
- **How we branch, commit and review:** [Contributing](https://github.com/BlockoutGames/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

## 🤝 Anything else

Partnerships, press and everything that is not above: [www.blockoutgames.com](https://www.blockoutgames.com).
