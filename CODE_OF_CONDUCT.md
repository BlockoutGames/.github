# Code of conduct

Blockout Games makes games for Roblox, and many of the people who play them are young. That shapes
how we treat each other, and how we treat our players.

This code applies to everyone who works with us: founders, members, contractors, testers and
partners. It applies in our repositories, our Roblox communities and games, our chats and
anywhere you represent the studio.

## How we work together

- **Be kind and assume good intent.** Critique the work, never the person.
- **Be direct and specific.** "This drops a frame on phones" helps; "this is bad" does not.
- **Share credit.** Name the people whose work you build on.
- **Welcome people.** Whatever their age, background, identity, experience or how they play.
- **Own your mistakes.** Say what happened and fix it. Nobody is punished for an honest mistake.

## How we treat players

- **Play by Roblox's rules.** Our games and our conduct follow the
  [Roblox Community Standards](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203313410-Roblox-Community-Standards)
  and [Terms of Use](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004647846-Roblox-Terms-of-Use).
- **Keep it on the platform.** Never ask a player to move to private messages or another app, and
  never ask for personal information, photos, passwords or money.
- **Protect player data.** Player data is used to run and improve the game, nothing else. It is
  never shared, sold or looked at out of curiosity.
- **Use staff powers for the game only.** Admin and moderation tools exist to keep a server fair
  and safe, never to settle a personal argument, show off, or favour friends.
- **Make fair games.** No misleading purchases, no pretending a random reward is better than it is,
  and odds are shown where Roblox's rules say they must be.

## Not okay

- Harassment, threats, insults, slurs or discriminatory jokes.
- Sexual language or imagery of any kind in studio spaces or games.
- Sharing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Using the studio's access, ranks or tools to harm a player, a member or a game.
- Leaking unreleased work, keys or private repositories.
- Encouraging or ignoring any of the above.

## Reporting

If something here is broken, or you are not sure whether it is, tell one of the founders
privately, or email **hello@blockoutgames.com**. You do not need proof, and you will not be in
trouble for raising it.

Reports are read only by the founders and kept confidential. If a report is about a founder, the
other founder handles it alone.

**If a child may be at risk, report it to Roblox straight away** with the in-game **Report** button
or [Roblox Support](https://www.roblox.com/support), and contact your local authorities if someone
is in immediate danger. Then tell us.

## What happens

Depending on what happened, the founders may:

1. talk it through and agree what changes,
2. give a written warning,
3. remove access to repositories, ranks or tools, for a while or for good,
4. end the working relationship, and report to Roblox where its rules were broken.
